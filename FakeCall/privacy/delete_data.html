App: Fake Call – Prank Call App
Last updated: October 2026

Summary: Fake Call does not use accounts and does not store any of your data on our servers. Everything the app saves — caller names/numbers, call history, settings — lives only on your device, and you are always in full control of deleting it.

1. No Account, No Server-Side Data

Fake Call does not require or support creating an account. We do not operate a backend server that stores your personal data. There is nothing on our end to send a deletion request for — all data the app uses is stored locally on your device only, inside the app's own private storage.

2. How to Delete Your Data

You can remove your data at any time using any of the following methods:

  1. Delete individual entries in-app — Open the app, go to the History tab or the Contacts tab, and tap the delete icon next to any entry you want removed. This deletes it immediately from the on-device database.
  2. Clear all app data — On your device, go to Settings → Apps → Fake Call → Storage → Clear Data (exact wording varies by Android version/manufacturer). This instantly erases all saved contacts, call history, and settings.
  3. Uninstall the app — Uninstalling Fake Call permanently deletes all of its locally stored data from your device.

3. What Happens After Deletion

Once you clear app data or uninstall, the deleted information cannot be recovered — there is no backup copy anywhere, because none was ever created outside your device.
