1. About This Policy

PrankMaster ("we", "us", "our") built the Fake Call – Prank Call App as a free Android application. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our app. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Access

The app does not collect or transmit personal information to any server. The following data is accessed or stored locally on your device only:

  • Contact names and phone numbers – You may manually type a caller name and number, or pick a contact from your address book. This information is stored in the app's local database solely to display on the fake-call screen. It is never uploaded or shared.
  • Fake call history – The app keeps a local log of fake calls you have made, answered, or rejected. This log is stored in an on-device database (Room/SQLite) and is never sent anywhere.
  • App settings – Your ringtone selection, dark mode preference, and scheduled alarm times are saved in Android SharedPreferences on your device only.

3. Permissions Explained

  • READ_CONTACTS – Allows you to pick a contact from your address book to use as the fake caller. Contacts data is never read in bulk, stored permanently, or transmitted off-device.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM – Used to fire the fake call at precisely the time you scheduled. The alarm is managed entirely by your device's AlarmManager.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW – Displays the incoming-call overlay on top of other apps when your device is shaken. Required for the shake-to-call feature to work from the background.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE – Keeps the shake-detection service running with a persistent, visible notification so you know it is active. You can stop it at any time from the notification.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – Shows the shake-service notification (Android 13+). No marketing or promotional notifications are sent.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – Restores any pending scheduled alarms after your device restarts, so a call you scheduled still arrives on time.
  • VIBRATE & WAKE_LOCK – Simulates the vibration and screen-on behavior of a real incoming call for a convincing prank.

4. Data Storage & Retention

All data (call history, saved contacts, settings) is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database and SharedPreferences. We have no access to this data. You can:

  • Delete individual history entries from the History screen in the app.
  • Remove all app data via Android Settings → Apps → Fake Call → Clear Data.
  • Uninstall the app to permanently delete all locally stored data.

5. Third-Party Libraries

The app uses the following open-source libraries, all of which operate entirely on-device:

  • Room (AndroidX) – Local database library for storing call history on your device.

The app does not include any advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services, or user-tracking libraries.
