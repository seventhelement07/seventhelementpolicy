Connect for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026  ·  Last updated 23 October 2026

The short version

  • Connect has no internet access. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it is not technically capable of sending your information anywhere.
  • Everything stays on your phone. Your contacts, reminders and call counts are saved in a database on the device and are never uploaded.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads. There is no sign-up, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting service in the app.
  • Uninstalling deletes everything. Removing Connect removes its database with it. Nothing is left behind on a server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

1Who this policy is from

Connect is an Android app published by SeventhElement (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with information on your device. It applies to the Connect app distributed through Google Play under the package name com.callscheduler.connect.

2What Connect does

Connect helps you stay in touch with people. You pick contacts from your phone, mark them as favourites, and set reminders to call them — daily, weekly, monthly, or on a specific date. When a reminder is due, the app shows a notification. Tapping it opens your phone’s dialer with the number filled in.

Connect does not place calls itself. It opens your normal dialer with the number ready, and you decide whether to press call. The app does not hold the CALL_PHONE permission.

3Information stored on your device

All of the following is written to a private database inside the app’s own storage area on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, and it is never transmitted.

WhatDetails storedWhy
Favourites Name, phone number and the contact’s system ID, for each contact you add So the app can show your chosen people and attach reminders to them
Reminders Which favourite, how often it repeats, the time of day, and when it is next due So the reminder can be scheduled and shown at the right time
Call history Name, phone number, how many times you opened the dialer for that person, and when you last did So the History tab can show who you contact most often

This history is Connect’s own count of times you tapped to call from inside the app. It is not your phone’s call log: Connect cannot see whether a call actually happened, how long it lasted, or any call you made outside the app.

4Permissions the app requests

Connect requests three permissions and no others.

PermissionWhat it is used for
READ_CONTACTS To show your contact list inside the app so you can choose who to be reminded about. Contacts are read on the device and shown to you. Only the contacts you explicitly mark as favourites are saved by Connect; the rest are read and discarded. Nothing is written back to your contacts, and nothing is uploaded.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show the reminder notification when a scheduled call is due. If you decline this permission, the app still works but cannot remind you.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To re-register your reminders after the phone restarts. Android discards scheduled alarms on reboot, so without this your reminders would silently stop after you restart your phone.

You can withdraw contacts or notification permission at any time in Android Settings. The app keeps working; the parts that depend on the permission stop.

5What Connect does not do

  • It does not send any information off your device. It has no network permission.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not create an account or ask for an email address, and it has no login.
  • It does not read your call log, SMS messages, location, camera, microphone or files.
  • It does not record calls and cannot hear or see anything about a call you make.
  • It does not modify, add or delete anything in your phone’s contacts.

6Sharing and selling

Connect shares no personal information with anyone, because it collects none to a server and has no way to transmit it. We do not sell, rent or trade any data. There are no third-party SDKs embedded in the app.

The one time information leaves Connect is when you tap a reminder and the app hands the phone number to your dialer app, at your request. From that point your dialer and your mobile network operator handle the call under their own terms.

7Backups

Connect’s database is deliberately excluded from Android’s automatic cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. Your favourites, reminders and history are not copied to Google Drive and do not move to a new phone. If you switch devices or reinstall the app, you will set your reminders up again.

8How long information is kept, and how to delete it

Everything is kept on your device until you remove it. You are always in control:

  • Delete one reminder by pressing and holding it in the Scheduled tab.
  • Remove a favourite in the Favorites tab; its reminders are deleted with it.
  • Delete a history entry by pressing and holding it in the History tab.
  • Clear everything at once with Settings › Apps › Connect › Storage › Clear data.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its database entirely.

9Children

Connect is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It collects nothing to a server from anyone, of any age.

10Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and similar regimes give you rights to access, correct, export and erase personal data held about you. Because Connect holds no data about you on any server and we have no copy of anything, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase on your behalf. The data is in your hands on your own device, and section 8 explains how to remove it.

11Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if a future version added a sync or backup feature — we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above before the new version is released. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes on Google Play.
