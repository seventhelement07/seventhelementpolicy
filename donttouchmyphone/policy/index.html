Privacy Policy — Don't Touch My Phone

Effective date: October 9, 2026

"Don't Touch My Phone" ("the App") is developed by SeventhElement ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how. The short version: the App works entirely on your device, has no internet access, and sends nothing to us or to any third party.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account, and does not collect, transmit, or have access to the internet at all — it does not request the INTERNET permission. Everything below stays on your device, inside the App's private storage, and is never sent anywhere.

DataPurposeStored where
PIN (as a one-way hash, never the raw PIN) Lets you disarm the alarm you set Local app storage only
Alarm sensitivity level, sound choice, trigger count, onboarding/rating flags Remembers your app settings and progress Local app storage only
Alarm event history (timestamp + trigger type, e.g. "Motion" or "Charger") Shown to you in the in-app Alarm History screen Local on-device database only
Accelerometer & proximity sensor readings Detects motion/movement in real time to trigger the alarm Processed in memory only — never stored or transmitted

None of the above data is linked to your identity, uploaded, backed up to a server we control, or shared with advertisers or analytics providers, because the App does not contain any advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Permissions the App Uses, and Why

Permission / FeatureWhy it's needed
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, WAKE_LOCK Keeps motion monitoring and the alarm sound running reliably while the screen is off
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Shows the "monitoring active" status notification required for foreground services
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Lets a previously-armed alarm resume after the device restarts
Camera flash (torch) hardware Flashes the LED as part of the alarm. The App never captures photos, video, or any camera feed — it only toggles the torch on/off.
Accelerometer / proximity sensors Detects that the phone has been picked up or moved

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the Google Play In-App Review API solely to show the standard Google Play rating prompt. This is a Google-operated feature governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not integrate any advertising network, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting service, and no other third party receives data from the App.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your PIN, settings, and alarm history remain on your device only, for as long as the App is installed. You can erase all of it at any time by:

  • Going to Android Settings → Apps → Don't Touch My Phone → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the App.

Either action permanently and immediately deletes all data described above, since nothing is stored outside your device.

5. Security

Your PIN is never stored in plain text — only a one-way cryptographic hash is kept, so it cannot be recovered or read back even from the device's own storage.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them. Since the App collects no information from the internet and has no account system, no information identifying a child is ever collected.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
