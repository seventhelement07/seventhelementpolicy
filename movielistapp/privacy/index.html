# Privacy Policy — MovieListApp **Last updated:** 9 October 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how **MovieListApp** ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. Please read it carefully. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below. ## Summary MovieListApp is a movie discovery app. **We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you.** The app has **no user accounts**, shows **no ads**, and uses **no analytics or tracking SDKs**. Everything you save stays **on your device**. ## Information We Do Not Collect We do **not** collect, request, or transmit any of the following: - Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information - Your location (precise or approximate) - Photos, videos, audio, contacts, calendar, SMS, or call logs - Advertising identifiers (AAID), device serial numbers, IMEI, or similar identifiers - Payment or financial information - Any other personal or sensitive data ## Information Stored Locally on Your Device To make the app work, the following is saved **only on your device** (using Android's local storage and an on-device database). It is **never** uploaded to us or any third party, and it is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app: - Movies you mark as **Favorites**, add to your **Watchlist**, or mark as **Watched** - **Custom lists** you create and the movies you add to them - The **genres** you select during onboarding (used to personalize recommendations) - App **preferences**, such as dark-mode and notification settings We have no access to this information. ## Network Requests to Third Parties The app uses the following third-party service to provide its core functionality: - **The Movie Database (TMDB)** — When you browse or search, the app sends requests to TMDB's public API (for example, a search term or a movie ID) to retrieve movie information, posters, cast, trailers, and streaming-availability data. These requests do not include any personal information about you. TMDB's privacy policy is available at https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy. *This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.* - **YouTube** — When you tap a trailer, the app opens the trailer link in your browser or the YouTube app. Your interaction is then governed by Google/YouTube's privacy policy. ## Notifications The app can show an optional weekly "New for You" notification about newly released movies in the genres you like. This feature is **off by default** and is controlled by a toggle in **Settings**. Notification content is generated on your device from public TMDB data; no personal data is involved. You can disable notifications at any time in the app or in your device settings. ## Permissions - **Internet** — required to fetch movie data from TMDB. - **Post Notifications** (Android 13+) — only requested if you enable the optional notifications feature, and only used to display the notifications described above. ## Children's Privacy MovieListApp is a general-audience app and is **not directed at children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app shows movie information from TMDB, some titles may be intended for mature audiences; parental discretion is advised. ## Data Security Because the app stores your saved items only on your own device and does not transmit personal data, your information is protected by your device's own security. Network requests to TMDB are made over HTTPS. ## Data Retention and Deletion All app data is stored locally. You can delete it at any time by removing items within the app, clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the app. There is no server-side copy to delete because we do not store your data. ## Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and publishing the new version at the policy URL linked inside the app. ## Contact If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer at: **moreshubham504@gmail.com**
