Privacy Policy

Application: ImageToText  ·  Last updated: June 2026

ImageToText is an offline text recognition app. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what is never collected or transmitted.

Information We Access

Camera. The app requests camera permission solely to capture photos for on-device text recognition. Captured images are never stored by the app unless you explicitly choose to save the extracted text. No photo is uploaded to any server.

Photo library. When you use the gallery picker to select an existing image, that image is accessed temporarily for text recognition only. The app does not copy, retain, or share it.

Saved text files. When you choose to save extracted text, it is stored in the app's private directory on your device: /Android/data/com.ocrai.imagetotext/files/OCR Files/. These files remain on your device under your full control. You can view, rename, or delete them at any time from within the app. They are never transmitted.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, account data)
  • No device identifiers (Advertising ID, IMEI, serial number)
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reports sent to external services

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google ML Kit for text recognition. ML Kit runs entirely on-device; your images are not sent to Google's servers as part of this feature. For more information see Google's Privacy Policy.

No advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs are included in the app.

Data Security

All data remains on your device. The app does not make any outbound network connections to collect or transmit user data. Cleartext HTTP traffic is blocked at the OS level.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. It contains no age-restricted content and is suitable for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
